z08 The problem

Three tools. Two exports.
One number nobody can check.

A protection register is only as good as the point inside it. Most are assembled by hand: a gazetteer in one window, imagery in another, a spreadsheet in a third. The coordinate that comes out the far side has no memory of how it was chosen — and a point attached to the wrong façade attaches the protection to the wrong building.
